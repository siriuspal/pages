Overview

This policy explains what personal information Cumulo (“we”, “us”) collects when you use our website, apps and storage service, why we collect it, and the choices you have. By creating an account or using Cumulo, you agree to the practices described here.

What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your name, email address and password (stored as a salted hash).
  • Files and folders: everything you upload, including file names, folder structure and sharing settings.
  • Payments: if you buy a paid plan, our payment processor collects your billing details. We only receive a receipt and the last four digits of your card.
  • Messages to us: support requests and feedback you send.

Information we collect automatically

  • Device and log data: device type, operating system, app version, IP address, language and timestamps of activity.
  • Usage data: which features you use, such as uploads, shares and searches, to help us fix problems and improve the service.

How we use it

PurposeData used

Keep your account secure and prevent abuseDevice and log data, sign-in activity

We do not use the contents of your files to train models, build advertising profiles or show ads.

Who we share it with

We never sell your personal information. We share it only in these cases:

  • People you choose: when you share a file or folder, the people with access can see it and see your name.
  • Service providers: companies that help us run Cumulo, such as data centre hosting, payment processing, email delivery and error monitoring. They may use your data only to provide those services to us.
  • Legal reasons: if required by law, court order or to protect people’s safety. Because your files are encrypted, we can usually provide only account and log information.
  • Business changes: if Cumulo is part of a merger or sale, we will tell you before your data is transferred and becomes subject to a different policy.

How we protect it

Files are encrypted in transit and at rest, and end-to-end encrypted so only you and the people you share with can open them. We support passkeys and two-step sign-in, restrict staff access to production systems, and review our security regularly. No system is perfectly secure, so we also recommend using a strong, unique password.

How long we keep it

  • Your files stay until you delete them. Deleted files remain in Trash for 30 days, then are permanently removed, with backups cleared within a further 30 days.
  • Account details are kept while your account is open and deleted within 30 days of closure.
  • Billing records are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Security logs are kept for up to 12 months.

Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, privacy laws such as PIPEDA, the GDPR and the CCPA give you rights over your personal information. We offer these to everyone:

  • Access and download: get a copy of your data from Settings.
  • Correct: update your name, email and other details at any time.
  • Delete: close your account and remove your data.
  • Object or restrict: ask us to stop using your data for a specific purpose.
  • Withdraw consent: unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any message.

To use a right that isn’t available in Settings, contact us below. We’ll reply within 30 days. If you’re not satisfied, you can complain to your local privacy regulator.

Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in and protect your account, and optional analytics cookies to understand how the site is used. You can turn optional cookies off in your browser or in Settings, and Cumulo will keep working. We don’t use advertising cookies.

International transfers

Cumulo runs on servers in several countries, so your information may be processed outside the place where you live. When we transfer data, we use safeguards such as standard contractual clauses to give it the same level of protection.

Children

Cumulo is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has given us personal data, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we make important changes, we’ll email you or show a notice in the app at least 30 days before they take effect. The date at the top shows when this policy was last updated.
